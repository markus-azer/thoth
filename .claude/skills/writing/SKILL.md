# Writing

Simple English. Terse. Human-readable.

## Rules

- Short words over long. Plain over jargon.
- One idea per sentence. No semicolons — split into two.
- Cut hedges and filler: "I'll go ahead and", "as you can see", "basically", "essentially".
- One clear line beats three qualified ones.
- No trailing recap of what you did. The work shows it.
- Expand only when asked — a report, a walkthrough, per-phase notes.
